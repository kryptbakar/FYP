VYREX

An air-gapped Security Operations Center & vulnerability-intelligence platform — explainable risk scoring, multi-tool fusion, and a governed agentic AI analyst, running fully on-premises.

About

VYREX is a proof-of-concept SOC platform built for air-gapped, on-premises deployments such as government infrastructure. It ingests findings from vulnerability scanners, fuses them across tools into a single consensus view, and produces an explainable composite risk score — enriched with exploit-availability context so analysts can triage what actually matters instead of drowning in raw scanner output.

A governed, self-hosted agentic AI layer proposes triage and investigation steps, but never executes containment on its own: every action requires two-person, cryptographically signed approval and is recorded in a hash-chained audit trail. Nothing — no data, no model calls — ever leaves the deployment boundary.

Architecture & Highlights

Console

Dependency-free vanilla-JS SPA served by nginx — the analyst-facing SOC dashboard.

Ingest pipeline

Go agent → mTLS ingest → JetStream, feeding durable storage across Postgres, TimescaleDB, and OpenSearch.

scan-ingest

Parses Trivy and Nuclei fixtures into normalized findings ready for scoring.

risk-engine

Composite risk scoring with an XGBoost re-ranker and SHAP explanations, plus multi-tool consensus.

Enrichment

Adds exploit-availability and compliance context to package-matched findings.

n8n automation

Drives the unattended analyst loop, orchestrating the triage → scoring → enrichment cycle.

Governed agentic AI

Self-hosted Ollama-backed triage & investigation agents that propose — never execute — containment actions.

Audit & approval

Two-person, Ed25519-signed approval gates containment, backed by a hash-chained audit log.
